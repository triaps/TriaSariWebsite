The Story

A Foundation Built
on Excellence

Born and raised in the vibrant city of Jakarta, Indonesia, Tria Puspita Sari built her academic foundation at the prestigious University of Indonesia, earning her bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering.

Over nine transformative years, she commanded projects spanning Real Estate, Infrastructure, Oil & Gas, and human development within the construction industry — cultivating a rare perspective that merges engineering precision with executive vision.

Driven by an insatiable pursuit of mastery, she relocated to Los Angeles, USA to pursue an MBA — bridging the technical rigour of civil engineering with the strategic acumen of global business development.

Today, Tria operates at the intersection of infrastructure and innovation, engineering business ecosystems that create lasting value for civil society.
